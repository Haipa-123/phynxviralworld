Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. About this project

Sphynx Viral Bot is an experimental content management project for authorized Sphynx cat videos on TikTok.

2. Website data

This informational website does not currently provide user registration or collect personal information through forms.

3. TikTok account information

The project is being developed to use TikTok's official authorization services. If enabled, it may access basic profile information and content publishing permissions explicitly authorized by the account owner.

4. Access tokens

During development, authorization tokens may be stored locally on the project operator's computer. They must not be published in public repositories or shared with unauthorized parties.

5. Video content

Video links and related content information may be organized locally for content management. Content is intended to be used only when the necessary rights and permissions have been obtained.

6. Third-party services

TikTok processes information according to its own privacy policy and terms. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.

7. Data security

Reasonable security measures will be implemented before any production deployment involving personal information or authorization tokens.

8. Your rights

Requests concerning personal information processed by this project may be submitted to its operator using the contact details published on the official website.

9. Updates

This policy will be updated before production deployment to accurately describe the implemented data processing activities.
